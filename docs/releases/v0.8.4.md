# Plainva 0.8.4

A journal for your day. This release brings together the changes since 0.8.3: a journal in the daily note with capture from anywhere, a task planner, widgets on the phone, voice notes, search and writing for every script — and fixes for synced vaults, including notes lost after a move in 0.8.3. Shared improvements apply to desktop and mobile.

If you sync a vault across devices, please update all of them (see *Sync and files* below).

## Journal

- Entries with a time (`- 14:05 Text`) go into the daily note. Capture from anywhere: **Ctrl/Cmd+Shift+J**, the **＋** menu, the command palette, the tray, the share target and, on the phone, a launcher shortcut and iOS quick actions.
- The journal view lists every day, as a list or as a card wall. The day header carries your mood, and **The day ends at** lets a day run past midnight (up to 06:00).
- Two devices appending to the same day no longer create a conflict copy.
- Voice notes play in the editor, reading mode, on the pinboard and in the journal, and can be recorded into the attachments folder. An optional location stamp adds your approximate position; it is off by default and set per device. Both are experimental.
- On the desktop an optional global quick capture opens a small capture window from anywhere (off by default; not available under Wayland).

## Tasks

- The planner opens on **Today**, with **Upcoming**, **Inbox**, **All** and **Done** next to it.
- Quick capture reads one sentence in ten languages: date, time, rhythm, priority (`!`, `!!`, `!!!`) and tags, shown as removable parts before anything is saved.
- Tasks carry a priority, a time and a reminder; `[/]` marks a task in progress and `[-]` a cancelled one.
- **New task** is available from the Android launcher, the desktop tray and the share sheet; on the phone a reminder offers **In 1 hour** and **Tomorrow morning**.
- Mirrored provider tasks are named after their title; an offer cleans up older names that carried an ID.

## Phone

- Widgets on Android and iPhone: **Today** with appointments and tasks, **Quick capture** for a task or the journal, and on the iPhone two lock-screen widgets. A widget holds no note text or path, and a locked encrypted workspace shows an empty widget. Ticking a task off in a widget is applied the next time Plainva opens (iOS 17 or later).
- A new note is named in the language of the app. Thanks for [#105](https://github.com/plainva/plainva/issues/105).
- On the iPhone the rows in **Bars & areas** can be used, and the folder screen tells loading, unreadable and empty apart. Thanks for [#104](https://github.com/plainva/plainva/issues/104).
- The calendar swipes in both directions; text fields in the settings keep their cursor.

## Notes, databases and reading

- Arabic, Hebrew and Persian run right to left, paragraph by paragraph, in the editor, reading mode and cards; the interface stays as it is. Thanks for [#111](https://github.com/plainva/plainva/issues/111).
- Search finds Japanese, Chinese and Thai words in the middle of a sentence, and so do occurrences, graph mentions and **Link**. Search results and backlinks can be sorted.
- Every database view has a search field. Ratings are an input type of their own, `file.day` places daily notes in calendar and timeline, and kanban cards carry the note colour.
- Pinboard entries are written in the full editor. Tags show as pills that open the notes with that tag; **Colour tags** tints them. Callouts render as one card.
- The desktop tab bar moves overflowing tabs into a menu, empty views offer their next step, and the daily note shows as a ring in the calendar.

## Mail, calendar and accounts

- New mail accounts can be set up on the desktop again; accounts left without a password by the failed setup are offered for cleanup.
- Saving an account again keeps its calendar and task lists and their selection; a network error no longer takes an account offline until the next sign-in.
- Events with an online meeting show **Join** with the service's name. Meeting notes can start from a template of your own.

## Sync and files

**Please update every device that works with the same vault.** A device still on 0.8.3 can delete a synced note after it is moved or renamed there, and it mirrors deletions without asking. If a note went missing that way, the [FAQ](https://github.com/plainva/plainva/blob/main/docs/user/en/FAQ.md#a-note-disappeared-after-i-moved-or-renamed-it) shows where to find it: Plainva's backups in `.plainva/backups/`, **Restore deleted files…**, and the trash of your system and your server.

- Moving or renaming a synced note in Plainva no longer deletes it at the next sync (WebDAV and S3 on every move; Google Drive, Dropbox and OneDrive on a full sync). Unsaved text moves along with the note or holds the move until it is saved. Thanks for [#113](https://github.com/plainva/plainva/issues/113).
- The sync never deletes local files because of a listing alone: a sample check and a per-file check with the provider come first, your own deletions do not return as a question from the cloud, and the deletion log can be undone.
- Paths are compared as one name in every Unicode spelling (for example folders created in Finder). Plainva writes into the spelling that exists and renames nothing; a folder that really holds both spellings shows the card **Two spellings, one file**. New folders are created inside the vault instead of at the account root (Google Drive, OneDrive, Dropbox, S3). Thanks for [#112](https://github.com/plainva/plainva/issues/112).
- Files moved outside Plainva keep their place: open notes, databases and images follow their file, unsaved text included. System files such as `.DS_Store` stay out of the vault. Thanks for [#110](https://github.com/plainva/plainva/issues/110).
- The comment sync accepts both ETag forms that proxies such as Traefik send and otherwise reads the file in full; a lasting sync problem is reported once instead of in every cycle.
- On first start Plainva converts its internal paths once and updates the search index; only notes that need it are re-read.

## Security and diagnostics

- Text parsing in the editor, import and sync runs in linear time; a lint guard keeps new patterns from backtracking.
- The comment notifier stays silent while a workspace is locked.
- The diagnostics export is always written in English.

Encrypted workspaces, the on-device calendar, voice notes and the location stamp remain experimental. These are specific corrections and improvements, not an independent security audit.
